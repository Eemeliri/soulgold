## Legendary Pokémon

#### Articuno

Found in Snowtop Mountain. Rock Climb is required.

#### Zapdos

Found at the top of Olivine Lighthouse after obtaining the eighth badge.

#### Moltres

Found in Victory Road.

#### Galarian Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres

Each bird can be purchased from the Route 50 Battle Café for 5 points.

#### Mewtwo

Found in Nameless Dungeon at Kitakami Border after defeating the Elite Four. Rock Climb is required.

#### Mew

After obtaining eight badges, visit Olivine City's port and find Blaine. Defeat him to unlock Faraway Island, where Mew can be found.

#### Raikou, Entei, and Suicune

All three begin roaming after the Burned Tower event.

#### Lugia

Choose the Silver Wing after the Rocket takeover. If you chose the Rainbow Wing instead, speak with the Director after defeating the Elite Four and prepare for a fight.

#### Shadow Lugia

Explore south of Johto to find the Abandoned Rocket Warehouse. Solve its puzzles to obtain a Dark Crystal, then bring it to the regular Lugia encounter.

If you have already caught Lugia, complete one round of Title Defense to reset the spawn. If you chose Ho-Oh during the main story, defeat the Director in the postgame to obtain the Silver Wing.

#### Ho-Oh

Choose the Rainbow Wing after the Rocket takeover. If you chose the Silver Wing instead, speak with the Director after defeating the Elite Four and prepare for a fight.

#### Celebi

Solve all eight Ruins of Alph puzzles: four slider puzzles and four wall puzzles. Enter a passage in B1F to find the GS Ball, take it to Kurt in Azalea Town, then visit the Ilex Forest shrine.

[spoiler=Ruins of Alph wall puzzle solutions]
- ESCAPE: Use an Escape Rope from the Bag.
- WATER: Use a Water Stone from the Bag.
- LIGHT: Use Flash from the party menu.
- HOOH: Bring Ho-Oh with you.
[/spoiler]

#### Regirock

Solve the braille puzzle in Vajra Desert West.

[spoiler=Regirock puzzle solution]
Bring Tyranitar to the wall.
[/spoiler]

#### Regice

Solve the braille puzzle in Snowtop Mountain.

[spoiler=Regice puzzle solution]
Bring Baxcalibur to the wall.
[/spoiler]

#### Registeel

Solve the braille puzzle in Railway Cave.

[spoiler=Registeel puzzle solution]
Bring Kommo-o to the wall.
[/spoiler]

#### Latias and Latios

Both are found in the Vajra Desert East temple during the postgame.

#### Kyogre

After completing the Darkrai event, your rival gives you the Dive HM. Surf south from Route 33 and take the first left to find a dive spot containing Kyogre.

#### Groudon

After completing the Darkrai event, your rival gives you the Dive HM. A dive spot on Route 50 leads to a small cave containing Groudon.

#### Rayquaza

Defeat Steven in the Kitakami house after becoming Champion, then visit Embedded Tower.

#### Jirachi

Complete the rival story at Mt. Silver.

#### Uxie

Found at the Rock Climb spot on Route 30 near the Berry Man's house.

#### Mesprit

Found at the Rock Climb spot on Route 47 near the final bridge before Route 48.

#### Azelf

Found at the Rock Climb spot in Mahogany Town.

#### Dialga, Palkia, and Giratina

Obtain Hoopa and solve all eight Ruins of Alph puzzles. Return to the secret room that held Celebi with Hoopa in your party, then interact with the back wall to find portals leading to Dialga, Palkia, and Giratina.

#### Primal Dialga
Have Grovyle, Dusknoir and Celebi in your party and interact with the Time Gear on Spear Pillar Summit.

#### Heatran

Explore the depths of Mt. Mortar after obtaining Waterfall and Rock Climb.

[spoiler=Heatran puzzle solution]
Select the lower line and the middle top.
[/spoiler]

#### Regigigas

Bring Regirock, Regice, and Registeel to the Mt. Silver back room.

#### Cresselia

After completing the rival story at Mt. Silver, speak with your rival in their house to begin the quest. Go to the Lake of Rage, speak with the Hidden Power Dude, then explore the north-east corner of the lake.

#### Phione

Fish with a Good Rod or Super Rod in the South Johto Sea area where only Shellos and Gastrodon can be encountered.

#### Manaphy

Evolve Phione at level 58.

#### Darkrai

After obtaining Cresselia, show it to your rival in their house. Go to Cherrygrove City and enter the inn with Cresselia in your party.

[spoiler=Darkrai inn puzzle solution]
1. Accept the Moon Key.
2. Go to your room at the top of the stairs, examine the bed, and choose not to sleep or resist sleeping.
3. Go outside, return to the lobby, and inspect the papers on the wall.
4. Inspect the Luxray trophy in the long hallway.
5. Inspect the old telephone on the front desk and call the number formed by the stack of papers.
6. Examine the piano to find a small key, then use it on the nearby safe.
7. Walk upstairs to find Darkrai.
[/spoiler]

#### Shaymin

Found in the Dream Garden, accessible from Tohjo Falls after obtaining Rock Climb.

#### Arceus

Reward from Oak in Radio Tower 2F for completing the Pokédex. Requires having caught all 615 non-legendary/non-mythical Pokémon.

#### Victini

After becoming Champion, speak with the old woman in the Kitakami Border house and defeat every Gym Leader rematch.

#### Cobalion

Found through the Route 33-side exit of Railway Cave after becoming Champion.

#### Terrakion

Found in Dark Cave North after becoming Champion.

#### Virizion

Found on Route 50.

#### Tornadus, Thundurus, Landorus, and Enamorus

Each force can be purchased from the Battle Café for 5 points. Your first force purchase also grants the Reveal Glass.

#### Meloetta

After becoming Champion, Meloetta can be found in the Ecruteak Dance Theater.

#### Genesect

Can be found in the Abandoned Rocket Warehouse after becoming Champion.

#### Eternal Floette

Obtain 60 trophies.

#### Diancie

Defeat every regular Battle Café opponent once, then claim Diancie from the café owner.

#### Hoopa

Complete the Rinto Village trade that gives Tinkaton to the scientist in the bottom house. After becoming Champion, explore the depths of Vajra Pyramid and prepare for several battles.

#### Magearna

After becoming Champion, enter the Rocket Arcade and speak with the burglar. Win 25 Arcade rounds and speak with him again to receive a key to the top floor of Goldenrod Apartment. Prepare for a fight.

#### Original Color Magearna

Obtain 100 trophies.

#### Marshadow

After obtaining the eighth badge, return to Elder Li in Sprout Tower and prepare for a battle. Afterwards, explore the Sprout Tower basement.

#### Tapus

Each tapu can be purchased from the Route 50 Battle Café for 5 points.

#### Poipole

Obtain 45 trophies.

#### Zeraora

Reach a 30-win streak in the Battle Tower to make Zeraora appear outside the tower.

#### Meltan

Trade for Tinkaton in Rinto Village.

#### Kubfu

Found in the Rock Climb cave in Blackthorn City. The evolution scrolls can be purchased from the Rocket Arcade for a very high price.

[spoiler=Kubfu cave solution]
Go down, up, left, up, left, down, right, right.
[/spoiler]

#### Zarude

Obtain 75 trophies.

#### Bloodmoon Ursaluna

Explore Kitakami Mountain to find the Black Mirror, then go to the Lost Woods.

[spoiler=Bloodmoon Ursaluna route solution]
Follow the Murkrow.
[/spoiler]

#### Paradox Pokémon

Explore South Johto's Meteor Island.

#### Chien-Pao

After obtaining Waterfall and Rock Climb, explore deeper into Ice Path.

#### Chi-Yu

Fish from the water fountain in Battle Cafe.

#### Walking Wake, Gouging Fire, and Raging Bolt

Register every past Paradox Pokémon in the Pokédex, then interact with the Unown wall at the back.

[spoiler=Past Paradox wall puzzle solution]
In the next room, bring Entei, Raikou, and Suicune to their respective walls to open them.
[/spoiler]

#### Iron Leaves, Iron Boulder, and Iron Crown

Register every future Paradox Pokémon in the Pokédex, then interact with the wall at the back.

[spoiler=Future Paradox wall puzzle solution]
Bring Virizion, Terrakion, and Cobalion to their respective walls to unlock them.
[/spoiler]

#### Koraidon/Miraidon

Both can be purchased from the Route 50 Battle Café for 25 points after obtaining all other Paradox Pokémon.

#### Fezandipiti

After obtaining Rock Climb, explore Kitakami.

#### Ogerpon

Explore Kitakami Mountain.
